## User Journey

Ben is a resident of Baker, and has been working very hard on his school work this semester, but wants a break from the endless grind of psets. Lucky for him, it's the middle of a long weekend, and he has time to spend not focusing on school. In order to clear his mind, Ben wants to play badminton on the courts just outside Baker, but the Z center is closed. He is just in luck! Ben remembers hearing that Baker has a wealth of items behind desk for Baker residents to check out and use.

Ben decides to check the Baker Belongings inventory to see what Baker Desk has available. He logs into the site, with his easily-remembered username and password, and clicks down to the Residents inventory view. Scrolling through the list he is surprised! There are a ton of items available for checkout. It's a little overwhelming, so he uses the 'Sports' flag to filter the inventory results. Only a hanful of items are displayed now. He scolls past the ice skates and the badminton nets. There's what he's looking for! The badminton sets are available for checkout. Pleased, Ben heads down from his room to Baker Desk.

Arriving at Baker Desk, Ben sees his friend Sam on shift. After exchanging greetings, he asks Sam if he can check out two set (one for his friend Herby) from behind desk. "Of course!" Sam says, "Let me just see your school id." Sam searches through the catalog of desk items with the AI augmented search function, finding the badminton set quickly. She clicks 'Checkout', and inputs Ben's kerb, which autofills by Sam typing in his name. Just like that, Ben has checked out his set.

Fast-forward a few days. Ben had a great time playing badminton, but accidently forgot to return the badminton set when he got back to Baker. A member of the housteam, Geraldine, now wants to check out the badminton set. She sees that Ben forgot to return the set, and contacts him through the management site. He is able to return the sets just in time, and will remember to return them sooner for the next time!
