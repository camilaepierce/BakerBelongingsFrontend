## User Journey

Cam is a resident of Baker, and has been working very hard on his school work this semester, but wants a break from the endless grind of psets. Lucky for him, it's the middle of a long weekend, and he has time to spend not focusing on school. In order to clear his mind, Cam wants to play spikeball on the courts just outside Baker, but the Z center is closed. He is just in luck! Cam remembers hearing that Baker has a wealth of items behind desk for Baker residents to check out and use.

Cam decides to check the Baker Belongings inventory to see what Baker Desk has available. He logs into the site, with his easily-remembered username and password, and clicks down to the Residents inventory view. Scrolling through the list he is surprised! There are a ton of items available for checkout. It's a little overwhelming, so he uses the 'Sports' flag to filter the inventory results. Only a hanful of items are displayed now. He scolls past the ice skates and the spikeball nets. There's what he's looking for! The spikeball sets are available for checkout. Pleased, Cam heads down from his room to Baker Desk.

Arriving at Baker Desk, Cam sees his friend Daisy on shift. After exchanging greetings, he asks Daisy if he can check out two set (one for his friend Herby) from behind desk. "Of course!" Daisy says, "Let me just see your school id." Daisy searches through the catalog of desk items with the AI augmented search function, finding the spikeball set quickly. She clicks 'Checkout', and inputs Cam's kerb, which autofills by Daisy typing in his name. Just like that, Cam has checked out his set.

Fast-forward a few days. Cam had a great time playing spikeball, but accidently forgot to return the spikeball set when he got back to Baker. A member of the housteam, Geraldine, now wants to check out the spikeball set. She sees that Cam forgot to return the set, and contacts him through the management site. He is able to return the sets just in time, and will remember to return them sooner for the next time!
